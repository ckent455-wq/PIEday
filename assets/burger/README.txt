PIEday burger patty assets

Editable Blender source: art/burger/burger_patties.blend. Source scene shows the two versions side by side; individual GLB exports are centered at the origin. Preview lights, floor and camera are excluded from exports.
Generator: art/burger/build_patties.py, run from repository root with Blender Python.
